Questions.

Do I need government contracting experience?

No. You do need to be willing to learn and to put in the work of bidding. The app explains each solicitation in plain English, walks you through SAM.gov registration and tells you which bids are realistic for a business your size.

Does it submit proposals for me?

No. It finds, prices and writes the bid, then waits for you. You review every proposal, make any changes and submit it to the agency yourself.

What is the difference between Core and Enterprise?

On Core, you choose a solicitation and upload it, and GovProposalPro does the rest, up to 10 proposals a month. Enterprise adds the GovCon Autopilot, which finds the solicitations, emails vendors for quotes, checks market prices and writes the proposals for you, plus 50 proposals a month.

How much is it?

Core is $97 a month and Enterprise is $297 a month, with hosting, support and updates included. Cancel anytime in Whop, and if it isn't for you, email us within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund.

How long until I win my first contract?

Honestly, it usually takes months, and nobody can guarantee a win. Most new contractors submit many bids before a first award, and many never win one. GovProposalPro makes each bid faster and more competitive. See our earnings disclaimer.
