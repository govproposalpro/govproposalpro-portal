Earnings Disclaimer

Last updated October 25, 2026

GovProposalPro is software that helps you find public contract opportunities and prepare bids. It is not a business opportunity, a job or a guaranteed way to make money. __ENTITY__ ("we") does not promise that you will win any contract or earn any amount.

What is typical

Winning government contracts takes time, effort, money and some luck. Most new contractors submit many bids over several months before a first award, and many never win one. We do not yet have data on what a typical GovProposalPro member earns, so we do not claim any typical result.

Wins and examples on our site

  • Contract wins shown on our site are reported by members of the Contract Bros community. They are not typical, they are not a prediction of your results, and they may reflect experience, relationships and effort beyond using GovProposalPro.
  • The "Do the math" calculator multiplies a contract value you choose by a margin you choose. It illustrates arithmetic on a contract you would still have to win and perform. It is not an estimate of what you will earn.
  • Any sample proposal, price or company shown in screenshots is fictional.

What affects your results

Your results depend on factors we do not control, including your registration and eligibility, the contracts you choose to bid, your pricing, your past performance, your subcontractors, agency budgets and competition, and how well you perform any contract you win. Contract revenue is not profit: you will have costs to perform the work.
