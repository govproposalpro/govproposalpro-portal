GovCon Autopilot

One press of Run.
Eight jobs done.

Each bid moves through the same steps a seasoned capture team would take, and you can watch every one in the live log.

  1. 1

    Learns your business

    Describe what you do, or what you want to do. It works out your NAICS codes, keywords and the contracts you should chase.

  2. 2

    Searches SAM.gov

    Searches SAM.gov with your own API key for open, biddable notices in your states with enough time left.

  3. 3

    Decides Go or No-Go

    Reads the notice and its attachments and skips the ones you can't win.

  4. 4

    Finds subcontractors

    Pulls SAM.gov registered vendors near the work, with their contact details.

  5. 5

    Emails for quotes

    Sends quote requests from your own mailbox and reads the replies when they come in.

    Looks up what agencies actually paid for similar work in USAspending award data.

    Prices at your margin

    Builds the price from quotes and market data, then applies your overhead and the profit margin you set.

  8. 8

    Writes the proposal

    A compliant, formatted proposal lands in your Ready lane. You review it and submit.

Questions.

What is the difference between Core and Enterprise?

On Core, you choose a solicitation and upload it, and GovProposalPro does the rest: the brief, the Go/No-Go score, subs, pricing and a formatted proposal, up to 10 a month. Enterprise adds the GovCon Autopilot, which finds the solicitations, emails vendors for quotes, checks market prices and writes the proposals for you, plus 50 Command Center proposals a month and the advanced tools.

Does the Autopilot submit proposals for me?

No. It finds, prices and writes the bid, then waits for you. You review every proposal, make any changes, submit it to the agency yourself and mark it submitted. Nothing goes to the government without you.

Why does the Autopilot use my own API keys?

So it can run as much as you want with no monthly cap. You add a free SAM.gov API key and a Claude API key from Anthropic during setup, and you pay Anthropic directly for what you use, with no markup from us. Keys are encrypted, never shown again in the app and can be replaced or removed at any time. The setup screen walks you through getting both.

Will it email vendors without asking me?

Only if you turn on automatic quote requests. Otherwise it lists the vendors it found and you send the requests with one click. Emails go from your own mailbox using an app password you can revoke whenever you like.

Do I need to be registered in SAM.gov?

You need an active SAM.gov registration to be awarded a federal contract. GovProposalPro includes a step-by-step SAM.gov checklist and flags it in every Go/No-Go check.

Where do the FAR clauses in my proposal come from?

Only from the solicitation and amendments you upload or that the Autopilot downloads from SAM.gov. The engine reads them and answers the clauses they contain. Anything it cannot confirm is left as a placeholder for you to fill in.

Can I edit what it writes?

Yes. Every proposal, capability statement, agreement and voucher can be edited, then exported as a formatted PDF or Word file with your logo.

How does pricing work?

You pay once for your edition: $897 for Core or $4,997 for Enterprise. Your edition also includes a monthly access plan that covers hosting, support and updates. Both run through Whop's secure checkout on this page.

What if I cancel?

Your edition is a one-time purchase, so it stays yours. If you are on a monthly plan, cancel anytime in Whop. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for, then it ends. Refunds follow the policy shown at checkout.

Do I need government contracting experience?

No. You do need to be willing to learn and to put in the work of bidding. The app explains each solicitation in plain English, walks you through SAM.gov registration and tells you which bids are realistic for a business your size.

How long until I win my first contract?

Honestly, it usually takes months, and nobody can guarantee a win. Most new contractors submit many bids before a first award, and many never win one. GovProposalPro makes each bid faster and more competitive. It can't make an agency choose you. See our earnings disclaimer.

Can I subcontract 100% of the work?

Usually not on a small business set-aside. Under FAR 52.219-14, the prime must keep a share of the work: for services, you can pay no more than 50% of what the government pays you to subcontractors that aren't similarly situated, and other limits apply to construction and supplies. The built-in checker shows where your bid stands before you submit.

How do I get my login?

After you purchase, you get an email with a link to create your login. Already a member? Sign in here.
