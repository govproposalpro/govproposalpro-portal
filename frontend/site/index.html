Why GovProposalPro

Bidding used to take weeks.
Now it takes an afternoon.

The old way

  • Reading 80 pages of solicitation and amendments to find what actually matters.
  • Guessing whether you even qualify for the set-aside.
  • Cold-calling subcontractors one by one and waiting on quotes.
  • Pricing on a napkin and hoping the margin holds.
  • A blank page and a deadline, with FAR clauses you have never seen.
  • Chasing payment because the invoice was not in the right format.

With GovProposalPro

  • A plain-English brief of the whole solicitation, with dates, clauses and the contracting officer.
  • A Go/No-Go score against your set-asides, NAICS codes and SAM.gov status.
  • SAM.gov registered subs found by NAICS and state, with a quote request sequence ready to send.
  • A price sheet with overhead and markup, so you see your profit before you bid.
  • A compliant draft that answers the clauses in your solicitation and flags what you need to confirm.
  • SF-1034 vouchers ready to upload, plus milestones so nothing slips after award.

Questions.

Do I need to be registered in SAM.gov?

You need an active SAM.gov registration to be awarded a federal contract. GovProposalPro includes a step-by-step SAM.gov checklist and flags it in every Go/No-Go check.

Where do the FAR clauses in my proposal come from?

Only from the solicitation and amendments you upload. The engine reads them and answers the clauses they contain. Anything it cannot confirm is left as a placeholder for you to fill in.

Can I edit what it writes?

Yes. Every proposal, capability statement, agreement and voucher can be edited, then exported to Word or saved as a PDF.

Is my company information private?

Your profile, documents and proposals are visible only to your account. Passwords are hashed, sessions are secure, and AI keys never reach the browser.

What is the difference between Core and Enterprise?

Core gives you 10 proposals a month and the full bid-to-invoice workflow. Enterprise raises that to 50 a month and adds the Matchmaker, Section M grading, teaming agreements, RFI drafting, your winning voice and advanced pricing.

How does pricing work?

Core Edition is a one-time $997 purchase and Enterprise Edition is a one-time $4,997 purchase. After checkout, you set up the monthly access plan for your edition, which keeps your access active and covers hosting, support and updates. Both are handled securely through Whop.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Checkout runs on Whop, which processes the payment. Your card details go to Whop, never to us.

After you purchase, you get an email with a link to create your login. Already a member? Sign in here.
